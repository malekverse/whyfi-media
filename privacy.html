Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Short version: WhyFi publisher only touches WhyFi's own YouTube channel. Its Google sign-in stays on the channel owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads, and this website collects nothing about you.

Who this covers

This policy covers WhyFi publisher, a private tool that the owner of the WhyFi channels uses to upload and schedule WhyFi's own videos, and this website. The tool isn't offered to the public: the only Google account that signs in to it is WhyFi's own.

What WhyFi publisher accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload WhyFi episodes, set their titles, descriptions, covers and publish dates, and mark the synthetic narrator voice.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected.

It doesn't read, change or delete anything else in the account, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

Where the data is kept

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only in a file on the channel owner's computer. There is no WhyFi server, so it's never sent anywhere except back to Google.
  • Information received from Google APIs isn't sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

WhyFi publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other platforms

The same videos are scheduled on TikTok, Instagram and X through Buffer, and the video files are briefly hosted on this site so Buffer can fetch them. None of that involves Google user data.

Visitors to this website

This site has no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and it loads nothing from other websites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it makes the stored token useless, and the owner deletes the local file.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date. Questions: reach WhyFi on X at @ask_whyfi or through the YouTube channel.
