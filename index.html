What WhyFi is

Short animated videos that answer one everyday question each: why you can't tickle yourself, why prices end in .99, and more. Every claim comes with a source.

Every frame is drawn with code. The narrator voice is synthetic, and the videos are labeled that way on each platform.

WhyFi publisher

WhyFi publisher is the small tool WhyFi uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel and schedule them. It runs on the channel owner's computer and isn't offered to anyone else.

It asks Google for permission to upload videos and to read the channel's basic details. Read the privacy policy for what it does with that access.
